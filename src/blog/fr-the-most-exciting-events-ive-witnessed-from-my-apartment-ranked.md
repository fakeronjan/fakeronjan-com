# {{ title }}

<p>I live in Riverdale in the Bronx, and have for 18 years. It’s a relatively quiet neighborhood where not much happens, since we are so far north of the hustle and bustle of Manhattan or the more lively parts of the Bronx.</p>
<p>That’s why when something happens, you notice! And in my case, I write it down.</p>
<p>We just had an earthquake in the northeast that was a <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/live/2024/04/05/us/earthquake-new-york#earthquake-new-york-boston-philadelphia" rel="noopener" target="_blank">4.8 magnitude in NYC</a>, so this easily makes the list of notable things to happen. And since I have a list, I might as well share it.</p>
<p>Without further ado, I present to you a completely comprehensive list of exciting things that I’ve witnessed from my apartment, in ascending order of excitement:</p>
<ul><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The time Hurricane Sandy hit NYC and we didn’t lose power, but we saw a barrel roll down the hill</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Many, many fireworks shooting off during the first year of the pandemic</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The time our cat Cersei busted out of a window onto the fire escape and emerged in our old apartment that she’d never been in before</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The time a couple loudly broke up during a massive rainstorm at 2 in the morning, with the man yelling “you’re ruining my life!”</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">Today’s earthquake</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The time water started pouring from the light fixture in a closet because someone two floors up left their sink on, causing $13k worth of damage</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The time a van crashed into our building and the main entrance awning collapsed</p></li><li><p class="" style="white-space:pre-wrap;">The time the school across the street exploded from a gas leak</p></li></ul>
<p>If this is a boring neighborhood in the city, I can only imagine what happens in an exciting one! Here’s to the next 18 years here in quiet, boring Riverdale.</p>

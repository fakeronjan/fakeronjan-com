# {{ title }}

<p>If you're reading this article, the chances are pretty good that you played <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NBA_Jam" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">NBA Jam</a> back in the early 1990s.  That game was an instant classic, <a href="http://sports.espn.go.com/espnmag/story?id=3668922" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">and the designers knew it</a>.  You could even argue that it is one of the time capsule creations of its era.  But we'll talk more about NBA Jam some other time.</p>
<p>With the new NBA season starting tonight (<a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p0j0lO7uQBo" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">YES!!</a>), I wanted to talk about <em>another </em>basketball game from that same era, a five-on-five game that is criminally under-shadowed by its much better known, much more popular older brother, <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tecmo_Super_Bowl" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">Tecmo Super Bowl</a>.</p>
<p>Enter Tecmo Super NBA Basketball.  I first played this game by renting it from Blockbuster several times in a row.  I didn't actually buy it until it came down in price -- all the way down to <em>ten cents</em> -- at Funcoland.  And seriously -- that's the best dime I ever spent!</p>
<p>This isn't the best basketball game ever made, or even of its era, but it's my personal favorite.  Let's count down five reasons why.</p>

<p>The menu music is borderline bad-ass, with its slower pace and prominent bassline.  Perfect for making roster changes and simming through the other team's games.</p>

<p><strong>#1: The roster</strong></p>
<p>Until <a href="http://espn.go.com/espn/thelife/videogames/blog/_/name/thegamer/id/6874034/the-15-legends-nba-2k12?readmore=fullstory" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">NBA 2K12</a> was released 2 years ago, Tecmo Super NBA Basketball had the best roster of players and teams of any basketball video game in history.  Seriously.</p>
<p>Larry Bird?  Magic Johnson?  Michael Jordan?  Check, check, check.</p>

<p>Tecmo Super NBA Basketball came out just as legends from the 1980s were on their way out and stars from the 1990s were making their mark, so it has a perfect confluence of talent.  As a result, the game starred the whole Dream Team (minus Christian Laettner) and Isiah Thomas.</p>
<p>The game also has two notable deceased players: Reggie Lewis, on the Celtics (above), and Drazen Petrovic, on the Nets.</p>
<p>It's unfortunate that the world never got to see Petrovic play to his full potential on the biggest stage.  The phenomenal <a href="http://www.amazon.com/Once-Brothers/dp/B008ZZO972" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">Once Brothers</a> 30-for-30 documentary highlighted just how good he was, and perhaps this game is another way that he can live on, forever.</p>
<p>Because sports games are literally outdated within 12 months of release, classic sports games are just as much a time capsule into <em>who was relevant</em> as they are a simulation of the rules of a sport.  And that's why I love this game so much -- it features the key players who defined the league for a generation.  I'll always go back to it.</p>
<p>Game on.</p>

# {{ title }}

<p>I love <em>The Simpsons</em>. I was younger than Lisa when it first aired; now I'm older than Homer. It trained my sense of humor, it taught me to punch up at institutions, and it has simply been a huge part of my life. Over the years, I've taken stock of my favorite episodes, both <a href="/blog/ua-off-topic-tuesday-countdown-favorite-simpsons-episodes/">overall</a> and the <a href="/blog/fr-top-5-treehouse-of-horror-episodes-from-the-simpsons/">Halloween specials</a>. I <a href="/blog/fr-simpsons-quality-over-time/">graphed the show's quality over time</a>, which directly inspired creating my <a href="/blog/fr-useless-analysis-from-uselessanalysiscom/">Useless Analysis website</a>. And I've spent countless hours arguing with friends over our favorites.</p>
<p>In July, I wanted to answer, definitively, the question of the show's best episode. I asked some of my closest friends from my Nielsen days and Simpsons meganerds to help me by voting in an elimination bracket of the show's <a href="https://www.theringer.com/2017/04/16/tv/top-100-simpsons-episodes-944725de5843" rel="noopener" target="_blank">top 100 episodes as designated by The Ringer</a> (chosen because it seemed like as reasonable a starting point as any). I set up a Slack bot to run the voting, and it pulled in episode descriptions and choice quotes for each. We even had a tiebreaker system so people could carry the episodes they cared the most about.</p>

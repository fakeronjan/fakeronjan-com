# {{ title }}

<p>In our last two posts, we saw that <a href="/blog/ua-how-high-can-the-warriors-fly/">the Warriors were off to the best start in modern NBA history</a>, but they'll need to keep up a torrid pace in order to match the 95-96 and 96-97 Bulls in wins and overall point margin. The 95-96 Bulls set the record for most wins in NBA history (72) and point margin in the 3-pt era, but <a href="/blog/ua-marginal-mayhem-a-warriors-follow-up/">the 96-97 Bulls actually started the season out being more dominant</a>.</p>
<p>The Warriors have since been defeated, but they still hold the record for best record through 25 games at 24-1.  After that loss, we had one final question.<br/><strong><br/>Do the Warriors win the same way that those Bulls teams did?<br/></strong><br/>We looked at point margin <em>by quarter</em> in the first 24 games of the season and compared this year's Warriors against the 96-97 Bulls through their first 24 games (they were 21-3). The results were interesting:</p>
<figure><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/blog/img/ua/do-the-warriors-win-like-the-96-97-bulls/5519119_orig.png"/></figure>
<p>Wow! The Warriors this year are fast starters - they tend to go up early in the 1st quarter and pull away as the game goes on. The Bulls from 96-97 were the opposite - they turtled in the 1st quarter and built their leads by closing out the first half strong and opening the second half strong.</p>
<p>This may be a fun view to monitor as the season goes on.</p>

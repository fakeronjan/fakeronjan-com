# {{ title }}

<p>NFL Films is known as the best in the sports highlight business, and for good reason.  They take already-compelling visuals, add deep-voiced narration, and top it off with brilliant, dramatic music.</p>
<p>I used to be obsessed with NFL Films.  Like, super obsessed.  My collection used to be even bigger, but here are the Denver Broncos highlight videos that I still have on VHS (despite having no VCR and not even being a Broncos fan anymore):</p>
<figure><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/blog/img/ua/countdown-rjs-favorite-nfl-films-music/4752083-272.jpg"/></figure>
<p>There was a stretch in my life in which I watched at least one of those videos every day.  Like, literally, <em>every day</em>.</p>
<p>I made two speeches in my Oral Communications college course related to NFL Films: the first being a "sales-oriented" speech where I sung the praises of NFL Films, the second being a "thank you" speech after I ... won the Grammy for "best musical score for a sports documentary" as composer for NFL Films.</p>
<p>Probably nerdiest of all, I ripped the audio of two of the above videos into MP3 format so I could listen to them while I worked the midnight shift shelving books at the Carnegie Mellon library.  (And yes, I still have those MP3s.)</p>
<p>Just imagine how excited I was when I heard that NFL Films was releasing a <a href="http://www.amazon.com/Autumn-Thunder-Years-Films-Music/dp/B0002VERQA" rel="noopener" target="_blank">10-CD box set of their music</a>!  Well, I can show you:</p>
<figure><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/blog/img/ua/countdown-rjs-favorite-nfl-films-music/2187337-663.png"/></figure>
<p>Yep, I bought it on day one!</p>
<p>If you're interested in more information about NFL Films music and its composers, <a href="http://www.nytimes.com/2005/09/11/nyregion/nyregionspecial2/11njNFL.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank">here's a great read</a>.  I can't really add much more perspective beyond that, but I figured I'd share my favorite tracks (I won't be including any of the old Sam Spence classics, as they've gotten their due over the years).</p>
<p><strong>Honorable Mention: <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FEXCpLOd1NE" rel="noopener" target="_blank">A New Game</a></strong><br/>This is the most famous NFL Films track, and it was the first one I ever heard when it was featured on the 1994 49ers championship VHS.</p>
<p><strong>Honorable Mention: Victory to the West</strong><br/>I wish I could find this track online -- it fits perfectly with the denouement of a victory as the clock ticks down.</p>
<p><strong>#5: <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0N0mS9wCrz0" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Heavy Hitters</a></strong><br/>This one is so different from what you typically associate with NFL Films, but I like how it comes together.</p>
<p><strong>#4: <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_inTQVsHK7A" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Return to Win</a></strong><br/>This one carries personal relevance, as it was featured in the 1997 Denver Broncos highlight video.</p>
<p><strong>#3: <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qHJsf2cTlHM" rel="noopener" target="_blank">75 Seasons Suite</a></strong><br/>The full 12 minutes are just beautiful, but my favorite movement is the section starting at the 5:00 mark.</p>
<p><strong>#2: <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TEXeWnJXRCc" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Drive to Glory</a></strong><br/>Perhaps the most intense NFL Films track, and it builds to a surprise at the 1:50 mark.</p>
<p><strong>#1: <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ukrBoLSEr2c" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Touchdown Suite</a> </strong>(just 20 seconds of a nine minute masterpiece)<br/>It is driving me crazy that I can't find the full suite online, because the section from 4:50 to 7:00 is the best work NFL Films has ever created.  What a miss.</p>
<p>-rj</p>

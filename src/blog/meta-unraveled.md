# {{ title }}

<p>Today, I did it again. I nuked my Facebook, Instagram, and Threads accounts to reduce my social media usage.</p>
<p>Social media has held varying levels of importance in my life over the past two decades.</p>
<p>Facebook was there for me when friends passed away. It was a way to connect with friends, classmates, and colleagues. It was there during some of the most exciting and most devastating political outcomes of my lifetime. Instagram was a delightful place to share photos with friends. See how everyone’s kids were growing up. How their vacations were going. It was great.</p>
<p>Both platforms were also places I spent more time than I’d like to admit. Places where I got riled up by algorithmically targeted content. Places I quit multiple times over before inevitably re-joining. Meta was a place I vowed I’d never work for before I took a life-changing job offer. A place that laid me off 15 months later.</p>
<p>Today, I watched <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2025-10-30/can-t-look-away-the-case-against-social-media-video"><em>Can’t Look Away</em></a> and decided it was time to walk away again. These platforms just seem to be net negatives for society, and I want to do my part in modeling healthy behavior for my middle-schooler son. (I also deleted Snapchat and TikTok today.)</p>
<p>We’ll see if it lasts this time. I hope it does.</p>
<p>Before deleting my accounts each time, I downloaded all of my data. Because I’m a nerd, I asked Claude to stitch it all together into a summary so I could see my full history with Meta’s platforms. Because I’m a super-nerd, I’m publishing it here for posterity. If you’re a nerd, I hope you enjoy it and get inspired to check your own history!</p>

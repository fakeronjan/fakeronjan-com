# {{ title }}

<p><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tecmo_Super_NBA_Basketball" rel="noopener" target="_blank">According to the internet</a>, <em>Tecmo Super NBA Basketball</em> came out on March 11, 1993 in the US. <a href="https://www.timeanddate.com/date/durationresult.html?d1=11&amp;d2=17&amp;m1=3&amp;m2=11&amp;ti=on&amp;y1=1993&amp;y2=2024" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Also according to the internet</a>, that means that the game is 1 billion seconds old today. This means that I have to pay tribute to this game, as it’s one of my all-time favorite video games that has frankly been lost to time a bit.</p>
<p><em>Tecmo Super NBA Basketball</em> suffers as a middle child of sorts. It was constantly overshadowed by its basketball contemporary <em>NBA Jam</em> which broke through as one of the biggest mainstream arcade hits of all time. And it was also overshadowed by its American football sister title, <em>Tecmo Super Bowl</em>, which broke through as one of the biggest mainstream console sports hits of its era. The very next year, the <em>NBA Live </em>series began in earnest and it would quickly differentiate itself as a more authentic and realistic 5-on-5 basketball game.</p>
<p>So yeah, <em>Tecmo Super NBA Basketball</em> is indeed lost to time. That said, while it’s not ever really considered among the greatest basketball games of all time, it may still be my favorite. Let’s count down the reasons why. <em>(This list was originally published at my old website on 10/29/2013.)</em></p>

<p>Maybe the quirky gameplay isn't as memorable as <em>Tecmo Super Bowl</em>'s, but it still stands out to me.</p>
<p><strong>#1: The roster</strong></p>
<p>Until <a href="http://espn.go.com/espn/thelife/videogames/blog/_/name/thegamer/id/6874034/the-15-legends-nba-2k12?readmore=fullstory" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title=""><em>NBA 2K12</em></a> was released 13 years ago, Tecmo Super NBA Basketball had the best roster of players and teams of any basketball video game in history.  Seriously.</p>
<p><em>Tecmo Super NBA Basketball</em> came out just as legends from the 1980s were on their way out and stars from the 1990s were making their mark, so it has a perfect confluence of talent. Larry Bird? Magic Johnson? Michael Jordan?  Check, check, check. The game actually starred the whole Dream Team (minus Christian Laettner) as well as famous Dream Team snub Isiah Thomas.</p>
<p>It also has two notable deceased players: Reggie Lewis, on the Celtics, and Drazen Petrovic, on the Nets. It's unfortunate that the world never got to see Petrovic play to his full potential on the biggest stage.  The phenomenal <a href="http://www.amazon.com/Once-Brothers/dp/B008ZZO972" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title=""><em>Once Brothers</em></a> 30-for-30 documentary highlighted just how good he was, and perhaps this game is another way that he can live on, forever.</p>
<p>Because sports games are literally outdated within 12 months of release, classic sports games are just as much a time capsule into <em>who was relevant</em> as they are a simulation of the rules of a sport.  And that's why I love this game so much -- it features the key players who defined the league for a generation.  I'll always go back to it.</p>

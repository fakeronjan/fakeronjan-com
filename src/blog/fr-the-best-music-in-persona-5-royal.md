# {{ title }}

<p>I played <em>Persona 5 Royal</em> <a href="/blog/fr-spending-sabbatical-in-high-school/">earlier on in my sabbatical</a> and ended up loving it <a href="/blog/fr-my-favorite-rpgs/">more than any other RPG</a> I’ve ever played. A big reason I love this game is the music. So I ranked my top 20 music tracks from the game.</p>
<p>Without further ado, here’s my list:</p>
<p><strong>20. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=woz5qvDdMRM" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Beneath the Mask</em></strong></a></p>
<p>“Oh, you’re back!” Yes I am, papa Sojiro. Yes I am.</p>
<p><strong>19. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vI9u_IeYJfI" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Tokyo Daylight</em></strong></a></p>
<p>What should I do today? Eat a giant burger? Some laundry? Darts? The world is my oyster.</p>
<p><strong>18. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V8mNJE2iIN0" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Price</em></strong></a></p>
<p>Not exactly what you picture when you’re pulling off a bank heist, but what a groove!</p>
<p><strong>17. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=it5wuAgf1Pw" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>When Mother Was There</em></strong></a></p>
<p>This one simply drives you forward. We have to help Futaba!</p>
<p><strong>16. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YTYkUvaEsio" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Ark</em></strong></a></p>
<p>Pretty epic! It also seems to call back to <em>Tokyo Daylight</em>.</p>
<p><strong>15. <em>Colors Flying High</em></strong></p>
<p>Not as earwormy as the original <em>Persona 5</em> intro song, but <em>Royal</em>’s new intro is still great!</p>
<div class="video-embed"><iframe allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen="" loading="lazy" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/vWWy7V9rCrA"></iframe></div>
<p><strong>14. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jXzsVT-V54k" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Butterfly Kiss</em></strong></a></p>
<p>Takemi is cool AF. Honestly woulda picked her as Joker’s romance option if it wasn’t <em>ridiculously creepy</em> <em>for a doctor to be dating a high schooler!</em></p>
<p><strong>13. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=69Km895ntNc" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>The Whims of Fate</em></strong></a></p>
<p>The only person in the game cooler than Takemi is Sae. Everything about the casino chapter was amazing.</p>
<p><strong>12. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LK20OK8VTUw" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Triumph</em></strong></a></p>
<p>The coolest “you win!” theme in video game history.</p>
<p><strong>11. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZNGqBDRJgvo" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Last Surprise</em></strong></a></p>
<p>This is a hell of a basic battle theme bop. The only problem is that it feels like a bummer when you hear this song because you didn’t ambush the opponent to earn <em>Take Over</em>. So technically, the opponent <em>did</em> “see it cooommmiiiiiinnnnnnngggggg!!!!!!!”</p>
<p><strong>10. <em>Wake Up, Get Up, Get Out There</em></strong></p>
<p>Damn. This one’s as relevant today as it ever has been. We <em>should</em> raise our collective voice against liars.</p>
<div class="video-embed"><iframe allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen="" loading="lazy" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/0jm8nnHqx80"></iframe></div>
<p><strong>9. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kexqSlkVTf0" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Gentle Madman</em></strong></a></p>
<p>Dat piano.</p>
<p><strong>8. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=41UPk-OeYQU" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Our Light</em></strong></a></p>
<p>What a way to roll credits on this beautiful song. You finally hear the soulful voice of Lyn in her native Japanese. Also, whether intentional or not, the hints of My Chemical Romance’s <em>Welcome to the Black Parade</em> hit hard.</p>
<p><strong>7. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XD-OFP-0H5g" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Rivers in the Desert</em></strong></a></p>
<p>Shit got real. It is indeed now or never.</p>
<p><strong>6. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LkuyO0cU3tQ" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>I Believe</em></strong></a></p>
<p>Everything about Maruki start to finish was amazing. One of the most philosophically provocative final opponents - not "big bad” - in gaming history.</p>
<p><strong>5. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k8z07I1fzbQ" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Throw Away Your Mask</em></strong></a></p>
<p>What I said in #6, copy-paste.</p>
<p><strong>4. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rJ2KB5LVdqA" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Take Over</em></strong></a></p>
<p>Easily the song you hear the most in <em>Persona 5 Royal</em> and it’s absolutely perfect as the “I’m in control of this battle” energetic theme.</p>
<p><strong>3. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rg2YOWTPq4s" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Life Goes On</em></strong></a></p>
<p>If only they played this bop during finals when I was in high school!</p>
<p><strong>2. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GOTDmQ_gGwI" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>No More What Ifs</em></strong></a></p>
<p>I barely heard this one in-game, but it’s the one I’ve probably listened to the most in real life. What a vibe.</p>
<p><strong>1. </strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CGwH6rZk7VM" rel="noopener" target="_blank"><strong><em>Life Will Change</em></strong></a></p>
<p>Yeah, it’s the basically the first thing you hear when you start playing the game. But this one gets you amped up and you never really forget it. This is the most confident video game theme I can think of. The driving introduction. The string flourishes. The bass breakdown. This is <em>Persona 5</em>, it knows what it is, it’s proud of it, and it wants you to know it. It’s the best thing in the game.</p>

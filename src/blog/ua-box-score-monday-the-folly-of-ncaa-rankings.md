# {{ title }}

<p>To keep our Box Score Monday streak going, here's an emergency, last-minute, super-short, Labor Day edition!</p>
<p>Today's game is from the first game of the 2002 Colorado Buffaloes' season against their intra-state rivals, the Colorado State Rams.  The Buffs were coming off of a super-successful 2001 season in which they won the Big XII conference, and they were rewarded in 2002 with a #7 pre-season ranking.  Meanwhile, Colorado State came into the matchup unranked.</p>
<p>The <a href="http://www.sports-reference.com/cfb/boxscores/2002-08-31-colorado.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">game</a> was very close, and Colorado State pulled off the upset to prevail, 19-14.  Naturally, the Rams would be rewarded for this win in <a href="http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2002_NCAA_Division_I-A_football_rankings" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">the rankings</a>, right?</p>
<p><u>September 2 AP Poll</u><br/>17. Colorado (0-1)<br/>19. Colorado State (2-0)</p>

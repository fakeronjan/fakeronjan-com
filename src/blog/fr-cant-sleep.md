# {{ title }}

<p>Can’t sleep.</p>
<p>Brain won’t stop engaging.</p>
<p>Engaging with never-ending anxiety.</p>
<p>Anxiety about the despot who won’t leave office.</p>
<p>Anxiety about the cynical destruction of truth and facts in our society.</p>
<p>Anxiety about the pandemic.</p>
<p>Anxiety about the well-being of my family who I haven’t seen for a year.</p>
<p>Anxiety about the family of a friend and colleague who suddenly and shockingly passed away in November.</p>
<p>Anxiety about my physical health. If it can happen to him, it will happen more easily to me. He was in shape. I’m not.</p>
<p>Anxiety about my mental health. Am I already gone? Are these normal thoughts? Is this life from now on? Every night?</p>
<p>Morning fast approaches.</p>
<p>Tears start flowing. Water pours out of my eyes, then out of my mouth. All the liquid in my body flows out. I feel my brain shrivel. I hear a “pop”. Then darkness.</p>
<p>That <em>was</em> sleep.</p>
<p>I’m exhausted. I need sleep. My body demands sleep. My mind demands a rest.</p>
<p>But I fear the dreams. I fear not waking up. Sleep is not a break. Sleep makes it worse.</p>
<p>Can’t sleep.</p>
<p>Maybe tonight is the night.</p>

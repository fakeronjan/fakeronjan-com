# {{ title }}

<p>Tomorrow, January 25th, is two very important things:<ol><li>It's my son's birthday</li><li>It's the first meeting of the year between the Golden State Warriors and the San Antonio Spurs, two teams that have demolished the league through the first half of the season</li></ol>Two months ago, we took a look at the then-undefeated Warriors and looked at a graph of wins and point margin through 18 games. At the time, <a href="/blog/ua-how-high-can-the-warriors-fly/">the Warriors had the best mark for each in the 3-point era</a>.</p>
<p>We decided to update this analysis in advance of tomorrow's Spurs-Warriors matchup:</p>
<figure><img alt="" loading="lazy" src="/blog/img/ua/the-spurs-and-warriors-are-both-making-history/7885884_orig.png"/></figure>
<p>Incredible! The Spurs, Warriors, and the 95-96 and 96-97 Bulls clearly separate from the rest of the crowd through 44 games, and this is a competitive set made up of the best NBA teams in the past 35 years.</p>
<p>The Spurs point differential of +638 is so video game absurd that if they were to finish the rest of the season even, that +638 would be the 24th-best in the 3-point era. Meanwhile, the Warriors still have the 2nd-best record at this point in NBA history, just one game shy of the 95-96 Bulls.</p>
<p>We took a look at the stats and found that, once again, <strong>point differential is more predictive of future wins than current wins</strong> when looking at this dataset. However, unlike previous analyses, the correlations are low. Some of these squads rested their starters when they clinched top seeds, and their end-of-season win totals ended up lower than you'd expect as a result.</p>
<p>The teams with the best record in games 45-82 were actually the 99-00 Lakers and 96-97 Jazz, as they each went 33-5 over their final 38 contests. The 95-96 Bulls and 96-97 Bulls won 31 and 30 games respectively in the same time period.</p>
<p>All of this is to say that if the Spurs and Warriors remain healthy, motivated, and on pace, they are both on track to make history. The all-time wins record of 72 and the 3-pt-era point differential record of +1,004 better look out!</p>
<p>Now let's hope the Spurs give my son a birthday win tomorrow. Go, Spurs, Go!</p>

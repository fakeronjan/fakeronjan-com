# {{ title }}

<p>It's been over half a year since our last Box Score Monday, the once-weekly segment detailing memorable games.  Well, now that the NBA Playoffs have begun, we're bringing this segment back.</p>
<p>We'll start with perhaps my favorite game ever -- <a href="http://www.basketball-reference.com/boxscores/pbp/200804190SAS.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">Game 1 from 2008's first round series between the Suns and the Spurs</a>.</p>
<p>The back story is well-known, but here's the run-down:<br/><ul><li>The Spurs came into the series as defending champions, and they'd won <a href="http://www.basketball-reference.com/playoffs/" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">3 of the past 5 titles</a></li><li>They beat the Phoenix Suns in each of those three title seasons (<a href="http://www.basketball-reference.com/playoffs/NBA_2003_WC1.html#SAS-PHO" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">2003</a>, <a href="http://www.basketball-reference.com/playoffs/NBA_2005_WCF.html#SAS-PHO" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">2005</a>, and <a href="http://www.basketball-reference.com/playoffs/NBA_2007_WCS.html#SAS-PHO" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">2007</a>)</li><li>They beat Steve Nash in each of those three title seasons (add in <a href="http://www.basketball-reference.com/playoffs/NBA_2003_WCF.html#SAS-DAL" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">2003 with the Mavs</a>)</li><li>Their 2007 series win was marred by <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LT_C7o5ZGM" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">a cheap shot foul</a> that led to <a href="http://www.azcentral.com/sports/suns/articles/2008/04/17/20080417suspensionsrecap.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">series-changing suspensions</a> that some felt cost Phoenix the series</li><li>The 2008 Spurs won 56 games and the Suns won 55 games ... yet this would be the 3 vs. 6 matchup in the playoffs, thanks to <a href="http://www.basketball-reference.com/leagues/NBA_2008.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">a very tight Western Conference</a></li><li>The Suns traded for none other than <a href="http://www.basketball-reference.com/players/o/onealsh01.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">Shaquille O'Neal</a> to give them some size against their longtime nemesis</li><li>The Suns won the 2008 season series, 3-1, setting the stage for potential revenge</li></ul><br/>And so the series began.  And basically ended.  In one memorable game.</p>
<p>The Suns started strong.  They scored the first five points of the contest and pushed their lead to 43-27 with four minutes left in the first half.  The Spurs closed the half on a run to cut the deficit to single digits, and the teams maintained a nerve-wracking shot-for-shot stalemate until about six minutes remained in the game.</p>
<p>The Spurs mounted a final charge and took a lead with two-and-a-half minutes remaining, but Phoenix rallied to hold a 93-90 lead with 20 seconds left.  One shot left.</p>
<p>Michael Finley delivers!  Overtime.</p>

<p>Steve Nash took on the scoring load in overtime, hitting a three that gave the Suns a five-point lead with just over two minutes remaining.  But the Spurs rallied to 104-101, and had the ball with 12.6 seconds remaining.</p>
<p>The next moment is one of my favorite sports memories ever.</p>
<div class="video-embed"><iframe allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen="" loading="lazy" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/trdsKp94Io0"></iframe></div>
<p>What a moment.  (And a <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l7ZZI5xCEs4" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">great NBA commercial</a> the next season.)</p>
<p>In double overtime, four times the Spurs took a two-point lead, four times the Suns tied it up.  The Spurs then took a three-point lead<em> </em>and this time it was the Suns who were down to their last shot with under 20 seconds to go.</p>
<p>Nash held up his end of the bargain, hitting the contest's third late-game-tying three, but Manu Ginobili hit a game winning layup with just seconds remaining.</p>
<div class="video-embed"><iframe allow="accelerometer; autoplay; clipboard-write; encrypted-media; gyroscope; picture-in-picture" allowfullscreen="" loading="lazy" src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/E0OFpWO-iao"></iframe></div>
<p>What. A. Game.  I've never seen another like it -- with the back stories, the tension, and the ridiculous game-tying shots.</p>
<p>The Spurs would go on to win the series in 5 games, and the Suns have never been the same.  Phoenix did finally beat San Antonio in the 2010 playoffs, sweeping the 7-seed Spurs, but Amare Stoudemire left for the Knicks after the season and Phoenix hasn't made the playoffs since.</p>
<p>Go, Spurs, Go.</p>

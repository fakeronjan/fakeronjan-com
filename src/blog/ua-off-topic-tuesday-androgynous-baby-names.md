<p>With the newest member of my family hitting the six-month mark over the weekend, I started reminiscing.  As part of that, I thought about the discussions that led to us eventually choosing his name, and once I started down that rabbit hole I went to the <a href="http://www.ssa.gov/oact/babynames/" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">U.S. baby name rankings</a> to see if there was anything interesting there.  <span style="background-">There has been a lot of fun analysis, with </span><a href="http://waitbutwhy.com/2013/12/how-to-name-baby.html" rel="noopener" style="background-" target="_blank" title="">this</a><span style="background-"> being my favorite article on the subject. </span></p>

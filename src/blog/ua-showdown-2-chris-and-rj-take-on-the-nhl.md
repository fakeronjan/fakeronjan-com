# {{ title }}

<p>Several weeks ago, we debuted our Showdown segment, where two of us battle on a topic and a winner is selected by a third party.  The first showdown featured a <a href="/blog/ua-showdown-lebron-and-durant-fantasy-draft/" title="">fantasy NBA draft</a> between LeBron James and Kevin Durant, where the two drafted teams to face each other in a seven-game series ... and <a href="/blog/ua-showdown-team-lebron-vs-team-durant/" title="">LeBron James won in 7 games</a>.</p>
<p>We're doing it again, this time for the NHL.  So, yeah, now it's not really novel -- it's exactly what the NHL does for its All Star Game already.  So what's our hook?  Well, no one on our staff really knows the NHL.  So Chris and I are going to wing it, and we will bring in our NHL expert and resident Rangers fan, Connor, to evaluate just how absurdly stupid we were.</p>
<p>The rules are basically the same as before: the two teams will face in seven games, and the winning team will split $10 million US or Canadian dollars.  Both owners will lay out their 4 offensive lines, 3 defensive pairings, and 2 goalies, and team chemistry counts.</p>
<p>Here's how our draft went.  Apologies in advance to NHL fans around the world.</p>

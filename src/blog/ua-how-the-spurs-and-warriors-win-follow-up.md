<p>A few weeks ago, we took a quick look at the Warriors and their <a href="/blog/ua-do-the-warriors-win-like-the-96-97-bulls/">point margin quarter-by-quarter</a> to see if they won <em>in the same manner</em> as the 1996-97 Chicago Bulls.</p>

<p>Since Chris has condemned the Mets to a worse finish than the Yankees after his bold proclamation, we've got to change the subject.</p>
<p>Hmm ... what's in the news?  Well, we learned yesterday that <a href="http://www.vox.com/2014/7/21/5923419/simpsons-world-streaming-fx-episode-clip" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">every episode ever will be available on-demand</a> in the Simpsons World app.  And we also learned that The Simpsons <a href="http://www.rollingstone.com/movies/news/save-me-the-krust-the-simpsons-likely-killing-off-krusty-the-clown-20140721" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">may be killing off Krusty the Clown</a>!</p>

<p>Previously in this space, I <a href="/blog/ua-countdown-the-simpsons-and-sports/" title="">counted down my favorite 20 sports references in Simpsons history</a>, and <a href="/blog/ua-off-topic-tuesday-how-much-is-your-penny-candy/" title="">I calculated how much Apu overcharges at the Kwik-E-Mart</a>.  So why not go for the brass ring and count down my favorite episodes, right?</p>
<p>Well, the good news is that I already put together my top 25 four years ago, and since every episode on that list is at least 15 years old at this point, I don't think I need to change anything, including the commentary!</p>
<p>So here we go!  (<a href="http://www.snpp.com/guides/ql.html" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="">Here's a good reference</a> if you need a reminder as to which episode is which.)</p>
<p><strong>25. Separate Vocations</strong><br/>I'm a big Lisa fan, as I'm sure you'll realize as we get further down the list. I'm also a sucker for Bart-Lisa moments, and loved how he sacrificed his new image to save hers.</p>
<p><strong>24. Marge vs. the Monorail</strong><br/>This might seem low for such a revered and classic episode. Frankly, if I hadn't seen it so many times, it would be higher on the list (as counterintuitive as that might be).</p>
<p><strong>23. Homer the Great</strong><br/>Ah, the Stonecutters. "Now let's all get drunk and play ping pong!" I also love the message: don't help others if you want to lead an organization.</p>
<p><strong>22. Fear of Flying</strong><br/>This episode's 2nd half isn't quite as strong as its 1st, when Homer gets banned from Moe's bar and meets Guy Incognito.</p>
<p><strong>21. A Streetcar Named Marge</strong><br/>I love the musical numbers on the show, and this episode might have had the most.</p>
<p><strong>20. Lisa the Iconoclast</strong><br/>Embiggens. Cromulent. Another great Lisa episode.</p>
<p><strong>19. The Old Man and the Lisa</strong><br/>Burns and Lisa, together at last. "Nothing evil. That's exactly the kind of radical thinking I need!" This is the first of many Mr. Burns episodes on this list.</p>
<p><strong>18. Burns Verkaufen der Kraftwerk</strong><br/>How can we leave out the episode that gave us The Land of Chocolate?! I also love Mr. Burns's negotiations to buy the plant back for half of what he sold it for.</p>
<p><strong>17. Blood Feud</strong><br/>"We'll get the Simpsons a present. An extravagant present. A mad, unthinkable, utterly impossible present! A frabulous, grabulous, zip-zoop-zabulous present!"</p>
<p><strong>16. Lisa's Sax</strong><br/>I particularly like the end of this episode, with the montage of Lisa playing the sax throughout all of the episodes. How can the family not afford an air conditioner, though?!</p>
<p><strong>15. A Star Is Burns</strong><br/>So many great entries into the film festival, but Man Getting Hit By Football tops them all. It was robbed!</p>
<p><strong>14. The City of New York vs. Homer Simpson</strong><br/>So many great moments, but I particularly like Homer's flashback to his first NYC trip with The Entertainer playing in the background.</p>
<p><strong>13. $pringfield</strong><br/>We're starting to get into "pure classic" zone here. This episode is fantastic from start ("Springfield: City On the Grow") to finish ("Well *you* have a gambling problem!").</p>
<p><strong>12. Treehouse of Horror V</strong><br/>Easily the best Treehouse of Horror in the series. I particularly love that Groundskeeper Willie gets an ax to the back in all three segments.</p>
<p><strong>11. Homer vs. the Eighteenth Amendment</strong><br/>"To alcohol! The cause of and solution to all of life's problems."</p>
<p><strong>10. The Springfield Files</strong><br/>This could have been a cheap promotional crossover, but I thought they did a fantastic job incorporating an X-Files storyline into The Simpsons and making it feel natural.</p>
<p><strong>9. Who Shot Mr. Burns? (Part One)</strong><br/>Burns at his best? It's in the argument, for sure. Part Two is great, too, but the setup of the mystery is particularly brilliant.</p>
<p><strong>8. A Fish Called Selma</strong><br/>Who would have thought an episode focusing on Selma and Troy would turn out so well?! Not to mention, the classic Planet of the Apes musical ("I hate every ape I see, from chimpan 'A' to chimpan 'Z'").</p>
<p><strong>7. Homer at the Bat</strong><br/>I love how Burns tempts fate: "Three misfortunes, that's possible. Seven misfortunates, there's an outside chance. But nine misfortunes? I'd like to see that!" Great song for the credits, too ("Talkin' Softball").</p>
<p><strong>6. Oh Brother, Where Art Thou?</strong><br/>I love that Homer can destroy an entire car company by himself. At least Bart liked The Homer!</p>
<p><strong>5. Cape Feare</strong><br/>Getting to the cream of the crop now. This is easily the best Sideshow Bob episode. I particularly like Homer's fantasy when he wants to be John Elway in the witness protection program.</p>
<p><strong>4. Last Exit to Springfield</strong><br/>It's pretty amazing that an episode about collective bargaining for a new labor agreement was so hilarious.</p>
<p><strong>3. Lisa's Substitute</strong><br/>This is probably the biggest surprise on the list. Again, I'm a big Lisa fan, and this episode did more to flesh out her relationship with Homer than any other. Just a classic.</p>
<p><strong>2. Rosebud</strong><br/>My favorite Burns-centric episode of them all. Homer as the gatekeeper to Burns's happiness? Fantastic. Also: "Moldy? Old? I'm gonna get something to eat!"</p>
<p><strong>1. You Only Move Twice</strong><br/>This has been my favorite episode ever since the first time I saw it. Hank Scorpio may be the best character who has ever been on the show. Ever.</p>
